I'm **The Data Doctor**: part database enthusiast, part Doctor Who fan. My real name is Sebastian Zok, and I have worked with IBM Db2 LUW since 2008, first as a consultant, then inside a retail IT department, and today as a Senior Database Administrator at a managed services provider.

## What I treat

Performance tuning, automation of recurring maintenance and, more and more, AI in the DBA's daily work.

## What you find here

Diagnoses for problems, prescriptions that work in practice, and regenerations for upgrades and migrations. Everything appears in English and German, and I keep to what I have tested myself.

## Outside the clinic

I volunteer for [IDUG](https://www.idug.org/), the International Db2 Users Group. Away from databases I'm a husband and father of twins, and a fan of tabletop role-playing, Doctor Who, Terry Pratchett and Star Wars.
