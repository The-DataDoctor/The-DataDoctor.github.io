Questions, corrections, ideas for a topic? I'm happy to hear from you.

- **LinkedIn:** [Sebastian Zok](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sebastian-zok-6a88b058/). A message or a comment on a post works well.
- **GitHub:** [The-DataDoctor](https://github.com/The-DataDoctor). Found a mistake in a post or in an example? Open an issue in the [repository of this blog](https://github.com/The-DataDoctor/The-DataDoctor.github.io/issues).
- **IDUG:** I volunteer for the [International Db2 Users Group](https://www.idug.org/). It is a good place to meet the Db2 community.

Please do not send me confidential data, passwords or production details. For hands-on help with a specific system, a post can only give general guidance.
