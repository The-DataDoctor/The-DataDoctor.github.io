## Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the site, GitHub processes technical data, for example your IP address, the date and time, the requested page and your browser type, in server log files. According to GitHub, data can be transferred to the USA. Please read [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure and reliable presentation of this blog (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

## What this website does not do

- It sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking or analytics service.
- It has no comment function and no forms.
- The search runs in your browser. Your search terms are not sent to me or to any server.
- It does not load fonts, scripts or images from third parties.

## External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, IDUG and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies. They only process data once you follow the link.

## Contact

If you write to me, for example on LinkedIn, by e-mail or in a GitHub issue, I process your message and the data in it only to answer you. GitHub issues are public. Please do not put personal or confidential data in them.

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under the GDPR. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, contact me as described on the [contact page]({{< relref "contact" >}}).
